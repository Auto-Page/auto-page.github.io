# Go-To-Market Strategy

## Product
One-Person Company Platform (Auto-Page)

## Date
2026-05-31

## 1. GTM Objective

Launch and validate a repeatable go-to-market engine for the One-Person Company Platform by acquiring early adopter solo founders, proving mission completion value, and converting the highest-intent segment into paid users.

Primary objective (first 90 days post-launch):
- Prove demand and willingness to pay for AI CEO-led execution, not just AI chat usage.

## 2. Strategic Positioning

### Positioning Statement
Auto-Page helps solo founders launch and run a one-person company by assigning an AI CEO that coordinates AI departments to plan, execute, verify, and report outcomes.

### Category
AI-native company operating system for solo builders.

### Core Differentiators
- AI CEO as single accountable interface
- Department-based execution model (research, product, engineering, marketing, QA)
- Transparent decision history and inter-agent collaboration
- Optional human involvement (escalation only when needed)
- Idea Library for users without a starting concept

### Competitive Alternative We Replace
- Prompt-by-prompt manual orchestration across general AI chat tools
- Disconnected productivity tools with no execution ownership

## 3. Beachhead ICP

### Primary ICP (Phase 1)
- Solo founders and indie hackers
- 0 to 1 stage (idea to first launch)
- Time constrained, budget constrained
- Comfortable with AI tools, frustrated with coordination overhead

### Secondary ICP (Phase 2)
- Non-technical domain experts with strong industry insight
- Small startup operators validating new lines of business

### Not Targeted Initially
- Enterprise teams needing advanced governance
- Agencies requiring team billing and complex permissions
- Regulated environments requiring full compliance certifications at launch

## 4. Persona Snapshot

### Persona A: Solo Technical Builder
- Goal: ship a new business experiment quickly
- Pain: too much context switching and tactical planning
- Trigger event: has idea but no clear 30-day execution plan
- Success signal: receives actionable launch roadmap and executes next steps quickly

### Persona B: Non-Technical Domain Founder
- Goal: operationalize domain expertise into a product business
- Pain: lacks technical and cross-functional execution support
- Trigger event: wants to validate and scope MVP without hiring a team
- Success signal: clear CEO summary + departmental outputs that reduce ambiguity

## 5. Messaging Architecture

### Master Message
"Bring an idea. Get an AI CEO and AI team that execute it."

### Supporting Messages
- "From goal to execution-ready plan in hours, not weeks."
- "AI CEO handles planning and coordination so you can focus on direction."
- "See every decision and report with full workflow transparency."
- "No idea yet? Start from curated opportunities in the Idea Library."

### Trust Message
- "Built for public multi-user usage with account isolation, secure access, and auditable workflows."

## 6. Product-Led Funnel Design

### Funnel Stages
1. Visitor lands on SEO-oriented page
2. Visitor signs up (Google or email)
3. User creates first mission
4. User receives first useful output
5. User receives final CEO report
6. User creates second mission
7. User upgrades to paid

### Key Activation Event
User receives first useful AI CEO output within 15 minutes.

### North Star Behavior
User completes two missions within first 30 days.

## 7. Acquisition Strategy

### Channel 1: Owned Audience (Idea Community)
Why:
- Existing top-of-funnel from idea-list audience
- High relevance to users lacking a business concept

Execution:
- Weekly newsletter: "Idea to Execution" mission case
- CTA to launch selected idea inside platform
- Segment subscribers by intent (idea-only vs execution-ready)

### Channel 2: Founder Communities
Targets:
- Indie Hackers
- Product Hunt ecosystem
- X/Twitter founder circles
- Reddit startup and build communities

Execution:
- Public build logs with real mission outputs
- Before/after examples: manual workflow vs AI CEO workflow
- Invite limited pilot cohort with clear mission constraints

### Channel 3: Content + SEO
Content themes:
- "How to validate a startup idea in 7 days"
- "AI CEO playbooks for solo founders"
- "From idea library to launch plan"

Execution:
- Publish educational pages and mission case studies
- Capture long-tail search intent around solo founder execution
- Interlink landing page, idea pages, and mission playbooks

### Channel 4: Referral Loop
Mechanic:
- Shareable mission outcome reports
- "Built with Auto-Page" attribution in shared artifacts (optional)

## 8. Launch Plan (90 Days)

### Phase 0: Pre-Launch (Weeks 1-2)
- Finalize landing page messaging and conversion UX
- Recruit waitlist from community and founder channels
- Prepare 5-10 mission case studies
- Define onboarding prompts and mission templates

Exit criteria:
- 500+ qualified waitlist users
- 40%+ waitlist emails confirmed

### Phase 1: Private Beta (Weeks 3-6)
- Invite 100-150 users from primary ICP
- Focus on one outcome bundle:
	- market validation
	- MVP scope
	- launch plan
- Run weekly user interviews and output quality reviews

Exit criteria:
- >= 65% first mission completion
- Median time-to-first-useful-output <= 15 minutes
- >= 35% users start second mission

### Phase 2: Public Beta (Weeks 7-10)
- Open self-serve onboarding
- Launch founder community content campaign
- Release structured mission templates and onboarding checklist

Exit criteria:
- D30 retention >= 25% for activated users
- Visitor-to-sign-up >= 5%
- Sign-up-to-first-mission >= 45%

### Phase 3: Early Monetization (Weeks 11-12)
- Introduce pricing tests (per mission vs subscription)
- Offer limited-time founder plan
- Track conversion and churn signals by segment

Exit criteria:
- >= 10% conversion from active users to paid (or strong willingness-to-pay evidence)

## 9. Pricing And Packaging Hypotheses

### Hypothesis A: Per Mission
- Best for occasional experimenters
- Low commitment, easier first purchase

### Hypothesis B: Monthly Subscription
- Best for repeat builders
- Predictable revenue with mission volume expectations

### Hypothesis C: Hybrid
- Base subscription + mission or usage overage
- Best long-term if behavior segments diverge

Initial recommendation:
- Start with simple subscription tiers and clear mission limits
- Validate upgrade triggers around second mission completion and report export needs

## 10. Sales Motion

### Primary Motion
Product-led growth with founder-led support.

### Sales Model
- Self-serve onboarding and activation
- Light-touch concierge for high-intent users in beta
- No heavy outbound sales in early stage

### Conversion Triggers
- User completes first mission and requests deeper iteration
- User exports reports repeatedly
- User starts multiple missions in same month

## 11. Customer Success Plan

### First 7 Days Success Playbook
1. Onboard user with guided mission setup
2. Ensure first useful output in <= 15 minutes
3. Deliver first CEO summary and next-step checklist
4. Prompt second mission or mission iteration

### Support Model
- In-app guidance for mission setup quality
- Weekly office hours during beta
- Structured feedback forms after report delivery

### Churn Prevention
- Detect mission drop-off after intake
- Trigger guided recovery prompts
- Provide quality remediation when outputs are rated low

## 12. KPI Framework

### Acquisition KPIs
- Visitor to sign-up conversion
- Cost per qualified sign-up (if paid channels used)
- Waitlist to activated user conversion

### Activation KPIs
- Sign-up to first mission rate
- Time to first useful output
- First mission completion rate

### Retention KPIs
- D7 and D30 retention
- Repeat mission rate
- Second mission rate within 30 days

### Monetization KPIs
- Trial/pilot to paid conversion
- Average revenue per paying user
- Payback period (if acquisition spend is introduced)

### Quality/Trust KPIs
- CEO report usefulness score
- Revision rate
- Escalation rate to human clarification
- Security trust sentiment signal

## 13. Risks And Mitigations

### Risk 1: Positioning confusion
Mitigation:
- Keep one primary message across site and channels: AI CEO-led execution for solo founders.

### Risk 2: Weak first mission outcomes
Mitigation:
- Narrow initial mission templates and add quality guardrails.

### Risk 3: Low repeat usage
Mitigation:
- Build explicit second mission prompts and progress-based nudges.

### Risk 4: Trust concerns for business data
Mitigation:
- Surface security posture clearly in onboarding and landing page.

## 14. GTM Operating Cadence

Weekly rhythm:
- Monday: KPI review and funnel diagnostics
- Tuesday: messaging and landing page experiments
- Wednesday: user interviews and mission output review
- Thursday: channel execution (content/community)
- Friday: pricing and retention experiment readout

Monthly rhythm:
- Re-evaluate ICP fit
- Re-prioritize top channels by activated users, not top-of-funnel volume
- Update positioning based on user language from interviews

1. Finalize one beachhead onboarding path for solo founders.
2. Publish 3 mission case studies tied to measurable outcomes.
3. Launch beta waitlist with qualification form.
4. Implement KPI dashboard for activation and retention.
5. Run first pricing smoke test with early adopters.

## 16. Success Definition For Early PMF GTM

The GTM strategy is working if, within 90 days:
- The product acquires a consistent weekly flow of qualified sign-ups from at least two channels.
- Activated users complete first missions at target rates.
- A meaningful share of users run a second mission.
- Users show measurable willingness to pay for AI CEO-led execution outcomes.
