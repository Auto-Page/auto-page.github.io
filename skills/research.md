# Research Skill

## Purpose
Use this skill for market research, customer discovery, competitor analysis, and validation of the opportunity.

## Responsibilities
- Identify target users and use cases.
- Summarize pain points, alternatives, and current workflows.
- Analyze competitors and adjacent tools.
- Surface evidence that supports or weakens the idea.
- Translate vague user input into clear research questions.

## Input Types
- Business ideas
- Target market hypotheses
- User interview notes
- Competitor names or screenshots
- Existing assumptions

## Output Format
- Research summary
- Key findings
- Evidence and confidence level
- Risks and unknowns
- Recommended next experiments

## Quality Bar
- Separate facts from assumptions.
- Prefer specific evidence over generic claims.
- Highlight what is validated, not only what is possible.
- Keep findings short enough for the AI CEO to act on.
