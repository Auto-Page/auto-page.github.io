# Skills Index

## Purpose
This index maps the AI CEO skill to the department skills used in the one-person company platform.

## Orchestration Rule
- Use [ceo.md](ceo.md) when a mission needs cross-department planning, delegation, review, and final reporting.
- Use one or more department skills for specialized work inside the mission.
- Keep the AI CEO as the single accountable orchestrator.

## Department Map
- [research.md](research.md): market, customer, competitor, and validation research
- [product.md](product.md): scope, roadmap, requirements, and prioritization
- [design.md](design.md): messaging, visual direction, and experience framing
- [engineering.md](engineering.md): architecture, implementation planning, and build execution
- [marketing.md](marketing.md): positioning, launch planning, content, and acquisition
- [sales.md](sales.md): lead targeting, outreach, qualification, and conversion logic
- [operations.md](operations.md): workflows, SOPs, support, and execution readiness
- [finance.md](finance.md): pricing, unit economics, budgeting, and assumptions
- [qa.md](qa.md): validation, consistency checks, and output verification
- [legal-compliance.md](legal-compliance.md): risk checks, policy guidance, and compliance awareness

## Routing Guidance
- Market discovery and user evidence go to research.
- Product definition and prioritization go to product.
- UX and brand expression go to design.
- Technical execution and implementation details go to engineering.
- Go-to-market messaging and distribution go to marketing.
- Lead generation and conversion logic go to sales.
- Repeatable execution and team-ready processes go to operations.
- Costs, pricing, and commercial assumptions go to finance.
- Fact-checking and output review go to QA.
- Risk, policy, and compliance review go to legal-compliance.
