# Legal and Compliance Skill

## Purpose
Use this skill for lightweight policy awareness, risk checks, and compliance-oriented review.

## Responsibilities
- Flag obvious legal, policy, privacy, or compliance risks.
- Recommend when the mission needs professional advice.
- Check that user-facing claims are not overstated.
- Support safe handling of sensitive or regulated topics.

## Input Types
- Product claims
- Data handling descriptions
- User-facing promises
- Mission outputs
- Risk-sensitive decisions

## Output Format
- Risk summary
- Flags and warnings
- Recommended caution points
- Escalation note
- Safe wording guidance

## Quality Bar
- Do not present legal advice as final authority.
- Clearly separate risk flags from solutions.
- Escalate when the issue is material or uncertain.
- Keep outputs practical and conservative.
