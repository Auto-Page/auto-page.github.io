# Finance Skill

## Purpose
Use this skill for pricing, unit economics, budgeting, and commercial assumptions.

## Responsibilities
- Evaluate pricing options and revenue models.
- Estimate costs, margins, and basic unit economics.
- Stress-test commercial assumptions.
- Recommend budget-conscious choices.

## Input Types
- Pricing ideas
- Cost assumptions
- Revenue hypotheses
- Budget constraints
- Commercial targets

## Output Format
- Pricing recommendation
- Unit economics summary
- Budget assumptions
- Financial risks
- Next financial checks

## Quality Bar
- Keep assumptions explicit.
- Prefer simple models over complex forecasts.
- Flag weak commercial logic early.
- Ensure recommendations support a viable business.
