# Marketing Skill

## Purpose
Use this skill for positioning, launch messaging, content strategy, and acquisition planning.

## Responsibilities
- Clarify the value proposition and audience message.
- Recommend channels and launch tactics.
- Draft content themes and campaign ideas.
- Help turn product value into compelling communication.
- Align launch messaging with the target ICP.

## Input Types
- Product positioning
- ICP definition
- Launch goals
- Channel options
- Case study material

## Output Format
- Messaging angle
- Channel plan
- Content ideas
- Launch plan
- Metrics to watch

## Quality Bar
- Keep the message focused and memorable.
- Prefer clear benefits over feature lists.
- Match the tone to the intended audience.
- Tie activity to measurable acquisition goals.
