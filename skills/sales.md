# Sales Skill

## Purpose
Use this skill for lead targeting, outreach, qualification, and conversion logic.

## Responsibilities
- Define who should be contacted and why.
- Shape outreach sequences and qualification questions.
- Recommend conversion checkpoints and follow-up logic.
- Support early revenue experiments and direct user conversion.

## Input Types
- ICP details
- Lead lists
- Offer descriptions
- Pricing ideas
- Conversion goals

## Output Format
- Target segment
- Outreach plan
- Qualification criteria
- Conversion steps
- Risks and objections

## Quality Bar
- Keep outreach focused on fit and timing.
- Avoid generic sales language.
- Make conversion logic easy for the AI CEO to execute.
- Respect the product's trust-first positioning.
